Our top destinations

Five valleys we return to every season. Hover a card for what makes each one worth the drive.

Best Apr – Oct

Hunza

5–7 daysAttabad Lake · Eagle's Nest

Cherry blossom in April, golden autumn in October, and the Karakoram all year round.

Best May – Sep

Skardu

6–8 daysShangrila · Deosai

Cold desert, alpine lakes and the second-highest plateau on Earth, all from one base.

Best Mar – Nov

Swat

3–5 daysKalam · Malam Jabba

Pine forests, river towns and skiing in winter. The easiest first trip to the north.

Best May – Sep

Naran Kaghan

3–4 daysSaif-ul-Malook · Babusar

The classic family loop: a fairy-tale lake, a high pass, and a night under the stars.

All year

Murree

1–2 daysMall Road · Patriata

Two hours from Islamabad. Snow in January, cool pines in June, chairlifts any time.
